Golden Rules
Never contact someone already converted
A committed membership — active OR pending activation — means no more automated pitches, ever. Checked live against Mariana Tek on every gather, plus a second check against our own converted log as a backstop.
Never double-text
Every candidate is checked against their real HighLevel conversation history, not just our own send log, before a draft goes out — catches the other automated system and anything staff sent directly too.
A real reply pauses automation
Anyone who's replied in the last 3 days is flagged as an active conversation and never auto-drafted on top of — a human handles it from there.
Per-segment cooldown, whichever send is more recent
A contact isn't re-touched in the same segment until their cooldown window passes, checked against whichever is more recent: our own log or any outside message to them.
Text first, email follow-up after 2 full days minimum
Follow-ups never fire sooner than 2 full days after the text, even for a trial ending imminently. No per-segment exceptions.
One product per message, always a direct close
Never "or" between two options. Every message ends on a concrete ask ("Want me to set that up for you?"), never a soft "no rush."
No price anchor in the opening text
First message is a soft, no-price interest check. Real numbers only once someone replies with interest, or in the follow-up email.
No em-dashes, ever
Enforced as a hard backstop at proposal time — a draft with an em-dash gets rejected automatically before it can be sent.
Every email ends with a signature
Texts don't need one; emails read as more formal and unsigned feels unfinished.
Lucas approves every send
Nothing goes out until he's reviewed the drafted table in chat. "Send" means exactly what's on the table just shown, never the whole pending queue.
